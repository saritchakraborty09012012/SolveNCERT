# SolveNCERT — AI Summary
> Free NCERT solutions for CBSE Class 9, updated for the 2026 Revised Syllabus.

## What is SolveNCERT?
SolveNCERT (solvencert-novexa.pages.dev) is a free educational platform providing human-verified NCERT solutions for CBSE Class 9 students in India. All content is aligned with the NCERT 2026 Revised Syllabus.

## Subjects Covered
- **Mathematics** — Ganita Manjari Part I (8 chapters): Orienting Yourself (Coordinates), Linear Polynomials, The World of Numbers, Algebraic Identities, Round and Round (Circles), Perimeter and Area, Probability, Sequences and Series
- **Science** — Exploration (13 chapters): Secondary Science intro, Cell, Tissues, Motion, Mixtures, Forces, Work & Energy, Atoms, Foundations of Matter, Sound Waves, Magnetism, Electricity, Environment
- **English** — Kaveri (prose and poetry chapters)
- **Advanced Mathematics** — Class IX CBSE Advanced Mathematics (6 chapters): Sets, Logarithms, Relations and Functions, Coordinate Geometry, Combinatorics, Exploring Some More Progressions

## Key Features
- Step-by-step solutions in CBSE board-exam format
- KaTeX-rendered maths with vertical fractions
- AI-powered learning assistant (Groq/Llama)
- PDF download of full chapter solutions
- Guest access: 2 free solution views, then free signup required
- Study Room for collaborative learning
